# ADR-004: Manual Merchant and Driver Approval (No Auto-Approval)

**Status**: Accepted
**Date**: January 2024
**Decision Makers**: Founder / Product Owner

---

## Context

When a merchant or driver registers on WASL, their account must be reviewed before they can use the platform. Three approaches were considered:

1. **Immediate auto-approval**: Account active as soon as registration is submitted
2. **Hybrid auto/manual**: Low-risk users auto-approved, high-risk reviewed manually
3. **Full manual approval**: Every account reviewed by an admin before activation

---

## Decision

**All merchant and driver accounts require manual admin approval before activation.**

No auto-approval in the MVP. Every registration is reviewed by an admin and explicitly approved or rejected.

---

## Rationale

### Why Manual Approval

**Quality control**: WASL's brand depends on reliable merchants and professional drivers. Manual review lets the admin assess whether an applicant meets the platform's standards before they interact with real customers.

**Fraud prevention**: Without document verification infrastructure, manual review is the primary guard against:
- Fake merchant accounts (competitors, bad actors)
- Drivers with invalid licenses or criminal history
- Duplicate accounts from previously rejected users

**Regulatory due diligence**: In the Egyptian market, demonstrating that WASL reviews users before onboarding reduces liability risk.

**Operational scale**: In the MVP phase (targeting 50–100 merchants and 100–200 drivers), the approval volume is manageable — estimated 2–5 approvals per day. This does not require automation.

**Simplicity**: No fraud detection logic, no document scanning service, no risk scoring. The admin reviews submitted information and makes a judgment call.

### Why Not Auto-Approval

Auto-approval would:
- Allow fraudulent merchants to create orders immediately
- Allow unqualified or unreliable drivers to accept deliveries
- Damage platform reputation before operational trust is established
- Create customer complaints that are harder to reverse

The cost of a bad actor on the platform (order fraud, driver abandonment) exceeds the friction of a 24-hour approval delay.

### Why Not Hybrid Auto/Manual

Hybrid approval requires:
- Risk scoring logic (what makes a user "low risk"?)
- Document verification (ID scan, business registration check)
- Threshold configuration and tuning

This is significant engineering work. At MVP scale, full manual review is simpler and equally effective.

---

## Approval Process

```
User registers
  → status = 'pending'
  → Cannot access platform features

Admin reviews (daily)
  → Checks: name, phone, business registration, vehicle details
  → Approves: status = 'approved', approved_at = NOW()
  → Rejects: status = 'rejected', rejection reason recorded

Approved user
  → Can log in and use their portal
  → action logged in audit_logs

Rejected user
  → Notified with reason
  → Can re-apply (new registration or contact admin)
```

---

## Approval Turnaround

Target: **Within 24 hours** of registration during MVP phase.

If approval backlog grows beyond 48 hours, escalate to increase admin capacity (second reviewer or batch processing).

---

## Consequences

**Positive:**
- Platform quality controlled from day one
- Fraud risk minimised without complex detection logic
- Admin maintains authority over who operates on the platform
- No technical complexity for onboarding flow

**Negative / Trade-offs:**
- Onboarding friction: Users wait up to 24 hours before first use
- Admin time cost: ~5–10 minutes per approval
- Does not scale beyond ~500 registrations per week without automation
- Manual errors possible (approving a bad actor, rejecting a good one)

---

## Mitigations

- Publish clear approval criteria publicly so applicants know what's expected
- Provide rejection with specific reason so applicants can reapply correctly
- Admin reviews pending queue daily (part of morning checklist in `OPERATIONS.md`)
- All approvals and rejections logged to `audit_logs` for accountability

---

## Future Review

Revisit this decision if:
- Approval backlog consistently exceeds 48 hours
- Team grows and approval load becomes a bottleneck
- A reliable document verification service (Onfido, Trulioo) is available and affordable
- Platform scale exceeds 500 new registrations per week

**Planned upgrade (Phase 2, Month 6+)**: Integrate a third-party KYC service for automated document verification. Retain manual review for edge cases.

---

## Alternatives Considered

### Immediate Auto-Approval
Rejected. Too high a risk of fraud and quality issues at platform launch, when WASL's reputation is most fragile.

### Automated KYC (document scanning)
Deferred. Available services (Onfido, Jumio, local Egyptian providers) require integration effort and per-user fees. Not justified for MVP with small user volumes.

### Tiered Approval (low-risk auto, high-risk manual)
Deferred. Requires risk scoring logic that would take significant development time. Revisit when fraud patterns are better understood from real data.
